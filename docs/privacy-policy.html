MBBS Handbook — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026 · App: MBBS Handbook (com.mbbshandbook.app) · Platform: Android

In one paragraph. MBBS Handbook is an offline-first handbook for medical students. You do not create an account and your study data never reaches us. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google processes limited device and advertising data for that — under your control and, where the law requires it, only after you consent. If you decline, ads are hidden and every feature keeps working.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

MBBS Handbook is an educational Android application published by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information when you install and use it. It covers the app itself and the advertising SDK it contains.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No accounts, sign-in, name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • No backend of ours: your bookmarks, progress, answers and scores are never uploaded to us.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social-login SDKs beyond the ads SDK described below.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, calendar, health or storage permissions. The app declares only INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and the Google Advertising ID permission required by the ads SDK.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device:

  • topics you have marked as read (study progress);
  • your bookmarks;
  • your best quiz scores;
  • your advertising consent choices (managed by Google's User Messaging Platform).

This information stays on the device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings. If Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android may include app settings in your own Google account backup; you control that in Android Settings. The app's backup rules exclude advertising and consent state, so consent is always collected on each device.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. When an ad is requested, the SDK may collect and share the following:

Sharing. Google processes this data for advertising and may share it with advertisers and measurement partners under its own policies. See Google's partner sites policy and AdMob privacy information. We do not sell personal information and we never receive your advertising ID.

Retention of advertising data is controlled by Google under its policies. Consent state is kept on your device until you change it or clear app data.

5. Your ad choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a Google-certified consent message is shown before personalised ads are requested. You may refuse or customise purposes and vendors. Without consent, ads are non-personalised or not shown — the handbook always works.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example CPRA), the applicable message is shown, including the ability to opt out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising and to limit use of sensitive personal information.
  • Changing your mind: when required, an “Ad privacy choices” button appears in the app to reopen the consent form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising and the related storage/access on your device, and on legitimate interests for non-personalised advertising, security, fraud prevention and providing the free app. You can withdraw consent at any time via “Ad privacy choices”.

7. International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside your country using the safeguards described in its privacy policy, including standard contractual clauses where required.

8. Security

Advertising traffic and the in-app policy link use HTTPS/TLS; the app blocks cleartext HTTP entirely. Local study data is protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on your region you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority (for example the ICO in the UK, your EU authority, the NDPC in Nigeria, or a US state attorney general). Because we hold no account data, most advertising requests are handled by Google — use the in-app choices or Google My Ad Center. For anything else, contact us below and we will respond within the period required by law.

10. Children

The app is intended for medical students and adult learners. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data has been processed through the ads SDK, contact us and we will help resolve it.

11. Medical disclaimer

MBBS Handbook is an educational study aid. It is not medical advice, not a clinical decision-support tool, and not a substitute for your curriculum, your supervisors or current local guidelines. Always verify doses, scores and protocols before clinical use.

12. Third-party links

The app links to this policy, to a public issue tracker and to third-party websites such as Google's privacy pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies that we do not control.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the effective date above. Material changes appear in the app's next release and on the Play Store listing.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: open an issue or use the developer contact email published on the MBBS Handbook Google Play listing.
