## Summary

MBBS Handbook is an offline-first educational app. Your bookmarks, reading
progress and quiz scores are stored locally on your device. We do not require an
account. The app uses Google AdMob to request banner and native advertisements. Google and its
partners may process device/advertising data as described below, subject to
your privacy choices and region.

## Data stored on your device

- Bookmarks, reading progress, quiz best scores and study-tool state.
- This data is stored locally and is not uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling
  the app removes it.

## Advertising and third-party SDKs

The app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform
(UMP). Depending on your consent choices and region, these SDKs may collect or
receive:

- Advertising ID and app set ID;
- IP address (used to infer coarse location);
- Device/app information and diagnostics;
- Ad impressions, clicks and other ad interactions;
- Consent/privacy choices.

Data is used for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement
and (where consented) personalization. Google's privacy information:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and
https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy.

You can change ad privacy choices in the app when the "Ad privacy choices"
button is shown, and reset/delete the Android advertising ID in Android settings.

## Consent

Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states), we ask for
consent before requesting ads. If consent is not available, ads are hidden and
the handbook remains usable.

## Children

The app is intended for medical students and adult learners. It is not directed
to children.

## Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Third-party SDKs may process data as described
above. Network traffic used by ads is encrypted in transit (TLS).

## Your rights and contact

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, delete, or object to
processing of personal data. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] for privacy requests.

## Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and revise the
effective date above.
