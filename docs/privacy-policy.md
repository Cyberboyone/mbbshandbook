# MBBS Handbook — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 9 November 2026
**App:** MBBS Handbook (`com.mbbshandbook.app`)
**Developer:** MBBS Handbook project (individual developer)
**Contact:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Cyberboyone/mbbshandbook/issues) or use the developer email shown on the Google Play listing.

> This is the canonical text. `docs/privacy-policy.html` publishes the same policy
> for hosting (GitHub Pages) and is the URL linked from the app and the Play Store
> listing.

## 1. Summary

MBBS Handbook is an offline-first educational handbook for medical students. You
do not create an account, log in, or send us your study data. Bookmarks, reading
progress and quiz scores are stored only on your device. The app shows
advertisements through Google AdMob; Google and its advertising partners process
limited device and advertising data for that purpose, subject to your choices and
your region.

## 2. Information we do not collect

- We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or any
  other account information.
- We do not operate a backend that receives your bookmarks, reading progress,
  quiz answers or notes.
- We do not include analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social-login SDKs
  of our own.
- We do not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, calendar,
  health-connect or storage permissions. The app requests only `INTERNET`,
  `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` and the Google Advertising ID permission used by the ads
  SDK.

## 3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in Android `SharedPreferences`:

- topics you have marked as read (study progress);
- your bookmarks;
- your best quiz scores;
- your advertising consent choices (managed by Google's User Messaging Platform).

This data never leaves your device through us. You can clear it at any time by
uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in Android Settings. If
Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android may include app settings in
your own Google account backup; you control that in Android Settings, and the
app's backup rules exclude advertising/consent state so consent is always
collected on each device.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP),
provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. When an ad is requested, the SDK
may collect and share:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Advertising ID (resettable) and App Set ID | Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention |
| IP address | Ad delivery and approximate (coarse) location inference — no GPS or precise location is requested |
| Device, OS and app information (model, language, app version, screen size) | Serving a compatible ad |
| Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, video views) | Measurement, billing and fraud prevention |
| Diagnostics (SDK errors, performance) | Reliability and abuse prevention |
| Your consent choices | To apply them to ad requests |

Purposes: delivering ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad
performance, preventing fraud and abuse, and — only where you consent —
personalising ads.

Sharing: Google processes this data as an independent controller/processor for
advertising, and may share it with advertisers and measurement partners under
its own policies. See Google's
[partner sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and [AdMob privacy information](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy).
The app itself does not sell personal information and does not receive your
advertising ID.

Retention is controlled by Google under its policies; consent state is kept on
your device until you change it or clear app data.

## 5. Your ad choices and consent

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before personalised ads are requested, you are
  shown a Google-certified consent message. You can refuse or customise purposes
  and vendors. If consent is not given, ads are requested in non-personalised
  form or not shown at all, and every handbook feature continues to work.
- **United States (state privacy laws, e.g. CPRA):** where required, you are
  shown the applicable message, including the ability to limit the use of
  sensitive personal information / opt out of sale or sharing for targeted
  advertising.
- **Other regions:** ads may be served without a consent form, subject to local
  law and Google policy.
- **Changing your mind:** when the SDK reports that privacy options are required,
  an **“Ad privacy choices”** button appears in the app. You can also reset or
  delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **Withdrawing consent:** use the same “Ad privacy choices” entry point; the app
  re-applies your choice to subsequent ad requests.

## 6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies we rely on: your consent (personalised advertising and
related storage/access), and legitimate interests (non-personalised advertising,
security and fraud prevention, and providing the free app). You can withdraw
consent at any time as described above.

## 7. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than yours, using the
safeguards described in Google's privacy policy (including standard contractual
clauses where required).

## 8. Security

All network traffic used by advertising and by the in-app privacy-policy link is
encrypted with HTTPS/TLS; the app disables cleartext HTTP entirely. Local study
data is protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of transmission or storage
is 100% secure.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete,
restrict or object to processing of personal data, to data portability, to
withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority
(for example the ICO in the UK, your EU supervisory authority, the NDPC in
Nigeria, or your state attorney general in the US).

Because we hold no account data, most requests concern advertising data held by
Google — you can exercise ad-related choices in-app or via
[Google's privacy controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/). For anything else,
contact us using the details below and we will respond within the time required
by applicable law.

We do not knowingly sell personal information, and we do not use or disclose
personal information for purposes other than those described here.

## 10. Children

The app is intended for medical students and other adult learners. It is not
directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided personal information through the ads SDK, contact us and we will help
resolve it.

## 11. Medical disclaimer

MBBS Handbook is an educational study aid. It is **not medical advice**, not a
clinical decision-support tool, and not a substitute for your curriculum, your
supervisors or current local guidelines. Always verify doses, scores and
protocols before clinical use.

## 12. Third-party links

The app links to this policy, to a public issue tracker and to third-party
websites (for example Google privacy pages). Those sites have their own privacy
policies, which we do not control.
