MBBS Handbook is a fast, offline-first study companion for medical students — a pocket handbook, question bank and clinical reference in one dark, distraction-free app.

Whether you are in preclinical years, on the wards or revising for finals, it gives you the guidance you actually need, without hunting through lecture notes.

BUILT FOR EVERY PHASE OF MED SCHOOL
• 11 handbook sections, 67 topics — getting started, preclinical, paraclinical, clinical rotations, clinical skills, exam success, ethics, wellbeing, careers
• Mnemonics vault and an Emergency quick reference you can open in seconds
• Section-by-section progress so you always know what is left

STUDY TOOLS THAT KEEP YOU MOVING
• 168 multiple-choice questions across 10 categories with instant feedback, explanations, shuffled 12-question rounds and saved best scores
• 68 flashcards in 6 decks — cranial nerves, normal values, antidotes, clinical vocabulary, paediatric milestones and obstetric emergencies — with a 3D flip and shuffle
• Clinical calculators: BMI, IV drip rate, pump rate, maintenance fluids (4-2-1), Glasgow Coma Scale and APGAR
• A searchable A–Z glossary of 64 medical terms

SPECIALTIES AND CAREER ROADMAPS
18 specialties across medical, surgical and acute/diagnostic groups. Each one covers what the work is really like, a day in the life, a visual MBBS → internship → residency → fellowship roadmap, key exams (WACP/NPMCN, MRCP/MRCS, USMLE routes), lifestyle and competitiveness, and what to start doing now.

MADE FOR REAL STUDENT LIFE
• Works offline — read, quiz and revise with no data connection
• Full-text search across every topic
• Bookmarks saved on your device
• Share any topic with a classmate
• Dark, edge-to-edge design that is easy on the eyes at 2am
• No account, no sign-in. Your reading progress and scores stay on your phone.

ONE HONEST NOTE
This is educational guidance for students. It is not medical advice and not a clinical decision tool — always follow your school's curriculum, your supervisors and current local guidelines, and verify every dose and protocol before you use it on a patient.

MBBS Handbook is free to use and supported by ads. Where the law requires it you are asked for consent first; if you decline, ads are hidden and every feature keeps working.
